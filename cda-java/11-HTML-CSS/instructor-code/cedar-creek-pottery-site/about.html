About Cedar Creek Pottery

Handmade stoneware, thrown and fired a few miles from where you're standing.

The studio

We work out of a converted barn beside the creek. Every piece is wheel-thrown, trimmed by hand, and glazed in small batches so no two come out of the kiln quite alike.

How a piece is made

  1. Wedge the clay to push out air pockets.
  2. Throw the form on the wheel.
  3. Trim the foot once the clay is leather-hard.
  4. Bisque fire, glaze, then glaze fire to cone 6.
