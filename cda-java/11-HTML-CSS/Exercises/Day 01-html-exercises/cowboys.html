About the Cowboys

The Dallas Cowboys are a professional American football team based in the Dallas–Fort Worth area of Texas. They compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member of the NFC East division.

The Cowboys play their home games at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.
